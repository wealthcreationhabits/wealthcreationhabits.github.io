Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

The Wealth Creation Habits Uploader is a private, single-user application operated by the owner of the "Wealth Creation Habits" YouTube channel.

What it accesses

With the channel owner's authorization, the app uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos to, and manage videos on, the owner's own YouTube channel. It accesses no other accounts and no other users' data.

What it stores

OAuth credentials are stored locally in the owner's secure credential storage. No personal data is collected from any third party, and no data is sold, shared, or transferred to any third party.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com → Security → Third-party access.
